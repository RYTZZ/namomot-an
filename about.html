OUR STORY

MADE WITH CARE, MADE FOR YOU.

A home-based handmade flower shop rooted in love and craftsmanship in Sorsogon City.

HOW IT BEGAN

A Passion for Making Meaningful Flowers

"Namomót-an started from my love for making flowers and creating something meaningful with my own hands."

I started making flowers to give to my friends whenever there was an occasion. I enjoyed making them, especially when I got to see how happy they were when they received the flowers.

Eventually, I thought that I could turn something I enjoy doing into a small business. It became a way for me to earn money while doing something I genuinely love.

THE DIFFERENCE

What Makes Namomót-an Special?

What makes Namomót-an special is that every bouquet is handmade with time, effort, and thought. I try to make each order feel personal instead of just making the same bouquet for everyone.

For me, it’s not only about how the flowers look, but also about the feeling behind why someone is giving them.

Every fuzzy wire stem, satin ribbon rose, and custom wrap is prepared to bring genuine warmth to your recipients.

THE NAME BEHIND THE CRAFT

NAMOMÓT-AN

"Namomót-an ta ka."

Namomót-an is derived from the expressive Bicol phrase "Namomót-an ta ka," which carries the heartfelt warmth of "Mahal kita," "Padaba kita," or simply "I love you."

We hold onto this sentiment in every single arrangement we fold, twist, and tie. Each piece is an everlasting keepsake that says what words cannot.

Mahal kita Padaba kita I love you

Everlasting Keepsakes
